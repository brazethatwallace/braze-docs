# Spanish style guide

## Grammatical gender for "Braze"

Avoid gendered articles directly before "Braze" when possible (prefer "de Braze", "con Braze"). When an article is required, match the gender of the head noun:

- "el Braze SDK" (because "el SDK")

## Terminology

- "Feature Flags" should be translated as **conmutador de características** (plural: conmutadores de características) to match the Braze product UI in Spanish. Do not use "banderas de características".

## Capitalization

- Only capitalize the first word of branded term phrases and proper noun phrases — do not title-case translated headings or feature names unless the target language convention requires it
- Inside a sentence, avoid English-style title case on common phrases (for example write *toma de decisiones*, not *Toma de decisiones*, when it is not a heading or proper name)

## Grammar and agreement

- Relative clauses must agree with their head noun in **number and person** (for example *los análisis … que incluyen*, not *… que incluye*, when *análisis* is plural).
- When you contrast two parallel subjects (for example *campañas* vs *Canvas*), keep **parallel structure** in each clause (*las campañas son … mientras que los Canvas son …*), not a singular verb with a plural subject.

## Register and tone

- Use informal Spanish (tú) — do not use usted
- The tone should be informal, friendly, and informative
- Content should sound approachable and conversational, as if explaining something to an acquaintance
