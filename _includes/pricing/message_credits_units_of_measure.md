{% alert note %}
Different products have different units of measure in reporting.

- **Agent Console:** Invocations
- **SMS:** Segments
- **MMS:** Sends
- **WhatsApp:** Messages delivered
- **RCS:** Segments delivered, Sends delivered
- **LINE:** Sends
- **KakaoTalk:** Sends

Lastly, carrier fees related to SMS, MMS, and RCS are billed separately (in arrears) and are not considered as part of this Message Credits SKU.
{% endalert %}
