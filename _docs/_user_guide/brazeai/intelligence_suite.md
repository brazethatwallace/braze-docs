# The Intelligence Suite

> The Braze Intelligence Suite automates decision-making with data-based insights. From delivery time to multivariate testing, you can use these features to create dynamic, cross-channel experiences that optimize at scale. The Intelligence Suite includes Intelligent Timing, Intelligent Channel, and Optimize with BrazeAI™.

## Use cases

The Intelligence Suite provides powerful features to analyze user history and campaign and Canvas performance, then make automatic adjustments to increase engagement, viewership, and conversions. For a few examples of how these features can benefit different industries, see the following use cases.

### eCommerce

- **Flash sales:** Use the [Intelligent Channel filter]({{site.baseurl}}/user_guide/brazeai/intelligence_suite/intelligent_channel) to study user history to identify the users who are more responsive to push notifications versus emails, then send push notifications and emails to the respective users. Optionally, select a specific channel for users who don't have enough data to determine their preferred channel.
- **Promotional banners:** Use [Optimize with BrazeAI™]({{site.baseurl}}/user_guide/brazeai/intelligence_suite/variant_selection) to analyze different promotional banners in a multi-send campaign and shift traffic toward the best-performing variant.

### Travel

- **Package offers:** Use [Optimize with BrazeAI™]({{site.baseurl}}/user_guide/messaging/canvas/create_a_canvas#optimize-canvas-variants-with-brazeai) to test travel package offers in a Canvas and gradually shift traffic to the best-performing variant.
- **Travel deals:** Use the Intelligent Channel filter to send personalized travel deals through a user's most active channel, such as email or SMS, maximizing the likelihood of them engaging with your messaging.

### Entertainment

- **New content promotion:** Use [Intelligent Timing]({{site.baseurl}}/user_guide/brazeai/intelligence_suite/intelligent_timing) to send notifications about new movies, shows, music, and other types of content when users are most likely to open your messaging.
- **In-game purchases:** Use [Optimize with BrazeAI™]({{site.baseurl}}/user_guide/brazeai/intelligence_suite/variant_selection) to test promotional messages and automatically prioritize the variants with the highest conversion rates.
