# RCS-supported sending countries and carrier coverage

> RCS coverage is the percentage of messages that brands can expect to be delivered as RCS to a given audience.

Two factors contribute to RCS coverage:

- **Device-level coverage:** RCS is enabled on nearly 100% of Android devices. RCS is available on iOS devices running iOS 18.1+. As of October 2024, approximately 68% of iOS devices have been updated (in other words, on iOS 18.1 or newer)
- **Cellular carrier coverage:** Because RCS is an emerging channel, cellular carriers are still working to support the protocol, and some countries have stronger coverage than others.
